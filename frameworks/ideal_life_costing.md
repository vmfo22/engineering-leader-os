# Ideal Lifestyle Costing

*Adapted from Tim Ferriss's lifestyle design concepts*

Most people never calculate what their dream life actually costs. They assume it's unattainable or work backward from their current income instead of forward from their desired life.

This exercise answers: **What does my ideal life actually cost?**

The answer is often less than you think—and having a number makes it achievable.

---

## Instructions

1. Dream without constraint first—describe what you want
2. Then price it out with realistic numbers
3. Calculate the monthly "nut" you need
4. Compare to current situation
5. Identify the gap and how to close it
