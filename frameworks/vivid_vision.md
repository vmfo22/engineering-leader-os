# Vivid Vision

*Based on Cameron Herold's Vivid Vision*

A Vivid Vision is a detailed description of your ideal future—not goals, but a snapshot of life as you want it to be. Write in present tense as if you're already living it.

The power is in the specificity. Vague visions create vague outcomes.

---

## Instructions

1. Pick a time horizon: 3 years from now
2. Write as if you're describing your life on that future date
3. Be specific—what do you see, feel, experience?
4. Include all dimensions of life, not just career
5. Don't edit yourself—write what you actually want
6. Review quarterly; rewrite annually
