# Leadership Philosophy Interview

A guided exploration of how you lead and why. This interview helps you articulate your leadership philosophy—not as a corporate exercise, but as genuine clarity about how you show up.

*Your leadership philosophy isn't what you think it should be. It's what's actually true about how you lead.*

---

## How to Use This

Be honest. This is for you, not for a performance review.

The goal: clarity on how you actually lead, what you believe, and where you want to grow.
