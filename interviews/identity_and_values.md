# Identity and Values Interview

A guided exploration of who you are and what matters to you. This isn't about who you should be—it's about getting clear on who you actually are.

*This interview helps you understand yourself more deeply. Return to it when you feel disconnected from yourself or when making major decisions.*

---

## How to Use This

Take your time. These questions deserve reflection, not quick answers.

You might want to:
- Work through a few questions per sitting
- Return over several days
- Talk through it with someone you trust

There are no wrong answers. Only honest ones.

---

## Part 1: Who You Are

**How would you describe yourself to someone who's never met you?**

*Not your job title or resume. Who are you?*




**What do people often get wrong about you?**

*The misperceptions. The things you've stopped trying to correct.*




**What do you know about yourself that took you a long time to learn?**




**What are you really good at—even if it feels like no big deal?**




**What comes easily to you that others find difficult?**




**What are you bad at that you've stopped pretending otherwise?**




---

## Part 2: Your Values

**When you're at your best, what values are you living?**

*Don't list what sounds good. List what's actually true.*




**What makes you angry? What crosses a line for you?**

*Anger often reveals what you care about deeply.*




**What are you unwilling to compromise on—even at significant personal cost?**




**Think of someone you deeply admire. What do you admire about them?**

*What they embody often reflects what you value.*




**Think of a time you felt disappointed in yourself. What value did you violate?**




---

## Part 3: Your Story

**What were the defining moments that shaped who you are?**

*The turning points. The before-and-after moments.*




**What did you learn from your family—good and not-so-good?**




**What beliefs did you hold earlier in life that you've since changed?**




**What's a failure or setback that ultimately made you stronger?**




**Is there a story you tell about yourself that might not be true anymore?**

*We all have self-narratives. Some are outdated. Which of yours might be?*




---

## Part 4: What Gives You Meaning

**What activities make you lose track of time?**




**When do you feel most alive?**




**What would you do even if you weren't paid for it?**




**What legacy do you want to leave?**

*Not what you think you should want. What do you actually want to be remembered for?*




**If you could only work on one problem for the rest of your life, what would it be?**




---

## Part 5: Your Needs

**What do you need to thrive?**

*Conditions, relationships, environments—what must be present?*




**What depletes you quickly if it's missing?**




**What do you need that you're not currently getting?**




**What do you need that you've been reluctant to ask for?**




---

## Part 6: Your Fears and Edges

**What are you afraid of?**

*Be honest. What keeps you up at night or makes your stomach tighten?*




**What do you avoid because it makes you uncomfortable?**




**Where do you play small because of fear?**




**What would you do if you weren't afraid of failure?**




**What would you do if you weren't afraid of what others think?**




---

## Part 7: Your Contradictions

**Where are you inconsistent? What contradictions live in you?**

*We all contain multitudes. Where do yours show up?*




**What do you say you value but don't actually prioritize?**




**Where does your behavior not match your self-image?**




**What's the gap between who you are and who you want to be?**
