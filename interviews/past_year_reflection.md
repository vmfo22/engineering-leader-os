# Past Year Reflection Interview

A guided conversation to process the year that was. Take your time. There's no rush.

*Designed to feel like a conversation with an executive coach—calm, curious, non-judgmental.*

---

## How to Use This

You can either:
1. Work through this alone, writing your answers
2. Ask Claude to walk you through it conversationally
3. Use it with a trusted friend, partner, or actual coach

The goal isn't to answer every question—it's to surface what matters.

---

## Part 1: The Overview

Let's start with the big picture.

**Tell me about the last year—highlights first. What went well?**

*Take a moment. What are you genuinely pleased about?*




**What are you proud of that no one else sees?**

*The private wins. The things you handled quietly. The growth only you know about.*




**What surprised you this year—in a good way?**




---

## Part 2: The Challenges

**Now let's talk about what was hard. What drained you the most?**

*Don't judge yourself. Just notice.*




**Where did things not go as planned?**




**What kept you up at night?**




**Was there a moment when you felt in over your head?**

*What was happening? How did you handle it?*




---

## Part 3: The Patterns

**Looking at both the highs and the lows—what patterns do you notice?**




**When were you at your best? What conditions were present?**




**When were you at your worst? What conditions created that?**




**What do these patterns tell you about yourself?**




---

## Part 4: The Honest Reckoning

*These questions might be uncomfortable. That's the point. Stay curious, not critical.*

**Where did you avoid hard decisions?**

*What were you putting off? What conversation didn't you have?*




**What did you tolerate that you shouldn't have?**

*People, situations, behaviors—what should have been addressed earlier?*




**Where did you let yourself or others down?**




**What would you not repeat under any circumstances?**

*Be specific. What's now on your "never again" list?*




**Is there anything you need to forgive yourself for?**




---

## Part 5: The Leadership Questions

*For your role as a leader specifically.*

**What did your best team members need from you that you didn't give?**

*Time? Clarity? Support? Challenge? Advocacy?*




**Where did you add the most leverage? The least?**




**What leadership mistakes did you make?**




**If your team had to describe your leadership this year in one sentence, what would they say?**




**What's the gap between the leader you want to be and the leader you were?**




---

## Part 6: The Relationships

**Who were the most important people in your year—professionally and personally?**




**Which relationships got stronger?**




**Which relationships got weaker or more distant?**




**Is there a conversation you need to have that you've been avoiding?**




**Who helped you that you haven't properly acknowledged?**




---

## Part 7: The Meaning

**What did this year mean in the larger arc of your life?**

*What chapter was this? What was its theme?*




**If this year repeated ten times, would you be satisfied?**

*Would you want to live this year again? What would need to change?*




**What mattered most to you this year? Did your actions reflect that?**




**What did you learn about what you actually want—not what you think you should want?**
