# Future Self Interview

A conversation with who you're becoming. This interview helps you design your future intentionally rather than letting it happen by default.

*Imagine you're talking to yourself 3-5 years from now—the version of you who has figured things out. What would they tell you?*

---

## How to Use This

Find a quiet place. Let your imagination work.

This isn't about making predictions—it's about getting clear on what you want and who you want to become.

Write freely. Don't censor yourself. You can edit later.

---
