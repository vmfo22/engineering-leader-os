## Principles I've Outgrown

Move principles here when they no longer fit, with a note on why. This is useful for tracking your evolution.

*Example:*
- *"Move fast and break things" — outgrew this because at scale, breaking things costs too much. Replaced with "Move fast on reversibles, slow on irreversibles."*
