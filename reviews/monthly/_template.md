## Deep Domain Scan

*15 minutes. The full shopkeeper walk-through. Once a month, look at everything.*

### People Lens

**Individual Wellbeing**
- Who is thriving right now? Why?
- Who is struggling or at risk? What are the signs?
- Anyone I've been avoiding a hard conversation with?
- Anyone who deserves recognition I haven't given?

**Team Health**
*Covered in Team Health Trends above. Add anything the numbers don't capture:*


**Leadership Relationships**
- How is my relationship with my manager? Any unspoken tension?
- Am I giving them what they need, or what I think they need?
- Any other senior leaders I need to invest in?

**Peer Relationships**
- Which peer relationships are strong? Which are transactional?
- Any peer leader/architect/PM I should be collaborating with but am not?
- Am I seen as a partner or a silo?

### Direction Lens

**Strategy / Direction**
- Is the current strategy still right? What has changed since we set it?
- Where is the biggest strategic risk?
- What bet are we making that I'm least confident in?
- Is there a strategic conversation I should be forcing but am not?

**Group Visibility**
- Does the broader org understand what my group does and why?
- When was the last time I proactively communicated our impact?
- Is anyone outside my org confused about our direction?

**Strategic Progress**
*Covered in Quarterly Goals above. One question to add:*
- Am I confusing activity with progress? Where?

### Execution Lens

**Operations / Process**
- Which ceremony or process has become theater?
- What operational friction are my team leads complaining about?
- Is there a tool, process, or workflow that needs to die?
- Where is toil accumulating?

**Delivery / Execution**
- Which deliverables am I most worried about?
- Is any team consistently underdelivering? Why?
- Are there delivery risks my team leads aren't seeing?
- Am I close enough to execution to notice problems early?
