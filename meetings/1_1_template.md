## Questions to Ask

*Standard questions to consider:*

**Opening:**
- What's on your mind?
- How are you doing—really?
- What's taking up most of your headspace?

**Work:**
- What's going well?
- What's frustrating you?
- Where are you stuck?
- What would help you move faster?

**Team:**
- How's your team doing?
- Anyone I should be worried about?
- Any tensions or conflicts?

**Career/Growth:**
- How's your development going?
- Are you getting what you need to grow?
- Any opportunities you're interested in?

**Support:**
- What do you need from me?
- Am I giving you enough context?
- Am I getting in your way?
- How can I help?

**Check-in:**
- How's your energy?
- How's your workload?
- Anything outside work affecting you?
