## Meeting Purpose

*Staff meetings serve to:*
- Align the leadership team
- Share critical information
- Make cross-team decisions
- Surface and solve cross-cutting issues
- Build leadership team cohesion

*Staff meetings are NOT for:*
- Status updates that could be async
- Topics that only involve 2 people
- Information that doesn't require discussion

## Staff Meeting Best Practices

### Before
- Send agenda in advance
- Mark topics as Inform/Discuss/Decide
- Include pre-read materials
- Assign time estimates to topics

### During
- Start on time
- Keep to time boxes
- Ensure everyone is heard
- Capture decisions and actions
- Park topics that go off track

### After
- Share notes and action items
- Follow up on commitments
- Evaluate meeting effectiveness
- Prepare for next meeting
