## During the Meeting

### To Remember

- Make eye contact (or look at camera)
- Pace yourself—slower than you think
- Pause for emphasis
- Check the room/chat for reactions
- Leave time for Q&A
- End on time

### To Avoid

- Reading slides verbatim
- Going over time
- Getting defensive on hard questions
- Saying "I don't know" without follow-up plan
- Rushing through important points

## All-Hands Best Practices

**Content:**
- Focus on 2-3 key messages
- Mix information with inspiration
- Include recognition
- Make it relevant to the audience

**Delivery:**
- Be authentic, not performative
- Show vulnerability when appropriate
- Use stories and examples
- Leave room for questions

**Format:**
- Respect people's time
- Start and end on time
- Make it worth attending in person
- Follow up in writing for those who missed it
