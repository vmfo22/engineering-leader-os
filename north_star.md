# North Star

Your north star is not a goal. It's a direction. Goals change. Tactics change. Your north star is the orientation that stays constant even when everything else shifts.

---

## The Three Questions

### 1. What kind of person do I want to become?

*Think about character, reputation, impact. Not titles or achievements — who you want to be.*



---

### 2. What kind of life do I want to live?

*Describe your ideal day, week, year. What does "a great day" look like? What do you optimize for?*



---

### 3. What impact do I want to have?

*At your company, in your industry, in the world. What would you be proud to look back on?*

## How to Use Your North Star

### Daily
Glance at it. Does today's priority align? If not, that's a signal.

### Weekly
During your weekly review, ask: "Did this week move me toward my north star or away from it?"

### Quarterly
In your quarterly review, check: "Are my goals aligned with my north star? Or am I chasing something that doesn't match who I want to be?"

### Annually
Rewrite it. You're not the same person you were a year ago. Your north star might need refinement.

### When Making Big Decisions
Ask: "Does this choice move me toward my north star?" If yes, courage. If no, caution.
